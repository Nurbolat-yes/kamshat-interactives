✚ FIRST AID ✚

First aid is immediate simple medical care given to a victim of an accident or an illness before professional medical treatment can be given. First aid can save lives.

It is based on the principle of the three Ps: prevent further injury, preserve life, and promote recovery.

When someone is lying unconscious at the scene of an accident, you should call for emergency services and make sure the area is safe.

Then, check the casualty. If they are breathing, try to put them in the recovery position. Do not adjust the position of their head and neck.

The recovery position keeps the victim's airways open and prevents choking while waiting for medical help.
