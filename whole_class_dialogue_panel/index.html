3. Descriptor & Assessment

Descriptor A learner identifies the main parts of a dialogue and suggests appropriate information.

Assessment Formative assessment.

Differentiation Support learners use sentence frames. More confident learners suggest one more question.

Resources Exercise 7, interactive board, dialogue structure card.
