About Our Research

This website is the practical product of a Grade 8 research project.

🔬 Research Question

"Can AI-personalized practice help 8th-grade students remember English vocabulary better?"

🎯 Research Goal

To find out whether personalized AI-assisted activities can help Grade 8 students learn and remember English vocabulary.

👥 Participants

22 Grade 8 students took part in the research.

📐 Research Design

Students worked with vocabulary from Unit 1 and Unit 2 using two different learning methods — traditional practice and AI-style personalized practice.

📋 Methods

  • Pre-test
  • Learning Activities
  • Post-test
  • Delayed Test
  • Student Survey
