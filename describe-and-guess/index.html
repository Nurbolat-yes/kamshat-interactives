DESCRIBE & GUESS 🎤

Pair speaking activity

Work with a partner! 👫 One person describes the mystery person. The other person listens and guesses. Take turns!

Learning objective 5.3.7.1: Use appropriate subject-specific vocabulary and syntax to talk about a limited range of general topics.

📋 How to play

  1. Choose a card.
  2. Look at the person.
  3. Describe the person.
  4. Do NOT say the name!
  5. Your partner guesses!
USE THESE SENTENCES
  • He/She has…
  • He/She has got…
  • His/Her hair is…
  • His/Her eyes are…
  • He/She looks…
